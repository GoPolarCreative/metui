About Us

Auckland Based.
Quality Focused.

Essential Lawn & Garden Keeper has been proudly serving Northshore, Central, South and West Auckland for over 3 years. Founded by Metui Vakaloloma, our mission is simple: deliver outstanding lawn and garden results that make your property shine — at a price that's genuinely affordable.

We care deeply about the work we do and treat every property as if it were our own. Whether you need a one-off tidy-up or ongoing maintenance, you'll always receive the same high standard of service and professionalism.
